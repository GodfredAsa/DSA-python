What to Expect in a 30-Minute Technical Interview
Given the short duration, the interview is likely to be focused and concise. Here's a breakdown of potential areas:

1. Problem-Solving and Coding
Objective: Assess your ability to write efficient, clean, and logical code.
Format:
A coding challenge or algorithmic problem (e.g., data structures, string manipulation, or arrays).
Tools: May involve a shared coding platform (like CoderPad, HackerRank, or Google Docs).
Example Question Topics:
Reverse a string or array.
Write a function to determine if a number is prime.
Implement a specific sorting algorithm or calculate the Fibonacci sequence.
2. Technical Knowledge
Objective: Test your familiarity with technologies, frameworks, or tools relevant to the role.
Topics:
Core concepts in programming languages you’ve mentioned on your resume.
Questions about frameworks, libraries, or databases you’ve worked with.
If applying for DevOps, Cloud, or Kubernetes roles, expect questions like:
"What is a Docker container, and how is it different from a virtual machine?"
"Explain the difference between stateful and stateless applications."
3. Past Experience & Projects
Objective: Understand your hands-on experience and problem-solving approach.
Questions:
“Tell me about a challenging technical problem you solved.”
“Explain a project you’ve worked on and the technologies you used.”
“What was a mistake you made on a project, and how did you handle it?”
4. System Design (if applicable)
If the role requires system-level thinking, they might ask about:
Designing a basic system (e.g., "Design a URL shortener").
Scalability, fault tolerance, and database design questions.
5. Behavioral/Soft Skills (Brief)
May include quick questions to gauge teamwork and communication:
“How do you handle disagreements on technical approaches with team members?”
“What do you do when you face a tight deadline?”
How to Prepare
Brush Up on Core Concepts:

Algorithms and data structures (arrays, strings, hash maps, recursion).
Frameworks and technologies relevant to the job description.
Revisit Your Resume:

Be ready to talk about your listed projects and responsibilities.
Highlight challenges, solutions, and technologies used.
Practice Live Coding:

Familiarize yourself with shared coding platforms.
Practice solving problems in 10–15 minutes to stay within time constraints.
Prepare Questions for the Interviewer:

Ask about team structure, tools, or upcoming challenges in the role.
